PRIVACY POLICY

Language: Português · English · ← Policies

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the game SWIPEQUIZ ("app", "game", "we"), developed by Brazil Games Studio, handles user data. SWIPEQUIZ is a casual card-swiping trivia and knowledge game for Android (package com.brazilgamestudio.swipequiz).

In short: no registration, personal email, or password is needed. Your answers, accuracy rates, unlocked categories, and score records are kept strictly on your smartphone.

Questions or requests regarding SWIPEQUIZ data?

See how to contact us. You can also delete your quiz history directly via your Android device settings.

1. Information handled by the game

1.1 Quiz answers and progress

Completed question decks, overall score, answering precision, and visual preferences are stored locally in the device app database.

1.2 Advertising (Google AdMob / UMP)

The game utilizes Google AdMob to display standard and rewarded advertisements. Player consent is managed via Google UMP (conforming to LGPD and GDPR). AdMob may process device advertising IDs and technical data for ad delivery and verification.

1.3 Optional purchases (Google Play Billing)

If you purchase optional question packs or remove ads, transactions are handled securely by Google Play. Financial or credit card data is never handled directly by the game.

2. How data is used

  • Track quiz scores and unlock new question packs;
  • Fulfill digital purchases bought via Google Play;
  • Display ads according to selected user consent choices;
  • Provide player assistance via support email.

3. Third-party partners

  • Google (AdMob, Google Play) — Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob — AdMob Data Disclosure
  • GitHub Pages — GitHub Privacy Statement

4. Data deletion

You can wipe all saved quiz data from your smartphone by navigating to Settings → Apps → SWIPEQUIZ → Storage → Clear Data.
