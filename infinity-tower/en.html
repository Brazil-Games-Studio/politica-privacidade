INFINITY TOWER

PRIVACY POLICY

Language: Português · English · ← Policies

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the game INFINITY TOWER ("app", "game", "we"), developed by Brazil Games Studio, handles player information. INFINITY TOWER is an arcade endless tower climbing and survival game for Android (package com.brazilgamestudio.infinitytower).

In short: no user accounts, social logins, or personal identifying information are required to play. Reached tower floors, unlocked upgrades, and personal best scores are saved on your local device. Advertising and purchases comply with Google Play privacy transparency guidelines.

Need assistance or data deletion regarding INFINITY TOWER?

See how to submit a request. You can also delete your gameplay records by clearing data or uninstalling the app in Android settings.

1. What data the game handles

1.1 Tower progress and device settings

The game stores highest floor reached, collected coins, unlocked perks, in-game achievements, and audio/graphics preferences directly on your device.

1.2 Advertising (Google AdMob / UMP)

Ads (interstitials, banners, or rewarded videos) may be served via Google AdMob. The Google UMP platform manages privacy consent (LGPD/GDPR). AdMob processes advertising IDs and basic technical parameters to serve ads and prevent fraudulent activity.

1.3 In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Item packs, coins, or ad-removal purchases are processed safely by Google Play Billing. Brazil Games Studio never accesses or stores user payment card details.

2. Purposes of data handling

  • Store tower climbing progress and statistics;
  • Fulfill optional purchases made through the app store;
  • Monetize gameplay fairly while respecting privacy rights;
  • Provide responsive player support when requested.

3. Third-party services

4. Deletion and control

4.1 Local data deletion

Navigate to Settings → Apps → INFINITY TOWER → Storage → Clear Data on your device to erase your local data.

INFINITY TOWER is rated for all ages and does not collect personal identifiers or build profiles of children under 13. If you suspect inadvertent submission, please reach out to our team.
