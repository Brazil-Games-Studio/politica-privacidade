PRIVACY POLICY

Language: Português · English · ← Policies

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the game SPACERUN ("app", "game", "we"), developed by Brazil Games Studio, handles user information. SPACERUN is a fast-paced space arcade runner for Android (package com.brazilgamestudio.spacerun).

In short: you can play without creating an account or providing sensitive personal information like emails or phone numbers. Your gameplay progress is stored locally on your device. For ads and optional in-app purchases, standard industry providers (Google AdMob and Google Play Billing) are utilized.

Questions about your data or deletion in SPACERUN?

See how to request data deletion. Since gameplay data is kept on your device, you can clear it yourself anytime through Android settings.

1. What data the game handles

1.1 Local progress and settings

SPACERUN stores your high scores, unlocked spaceships, collected coins, and audio/control preferences locally on your device. This data remains strictly on your device and will be removed if the app is uninstalled or cleared.

1.2 Advertising and consent

The game may display ads via Google AdMob utilizing the Google UMP consent platform (compliant with LGPD and GDPR). AdMob may process advertising identifiers (Advertising ID), IP address, and device diagnostics for ad serving, measurement, and fraud prevention.

1.3 In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

If you purchase optional items (such as cosmetics or coin packs), payments are processed directly by Google Play. We do not receive or store any credit card or financial credentials.

1.4 Support and inquiries

If you contact us via email for support or feedback, we will process your email address and message solely to assist you.

2. How we use your data

  • Deliver core gameplay and save your progress locally;
  • Serve ads respecting your user consent choices;
  • Fulfill and maintain optional digital purchases through Google Play;
  • Provide customer support and resolve technical issues.

3. Third-party disclosures

We do not sell personal information. Technical data necessary for the app's operation is shared only with verified service providers:

  • Google (AdMob, Google Play) — Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob — AdMob Data Disclosure
  • GitHub Pages (Hosting for this policy) — GitHub Privacy Statement

4. Data retention and deletion

4.1 Local data

You can clear all stored game data anytime via Settings → Apps → SPACERUN → Storage → Clear Data on your Android device, or simply by uninstalling the application.

5. Children's privacy

SPACERUN is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes personal data was provided inadvertently, please contact us for immediate deletion.

6. Security and policy changes

We maintain standard technical safeguards to protect data. We may update this policy periodically to reflect game updates or regulatory changes.
