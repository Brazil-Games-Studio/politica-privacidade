PRIVACY POLICY

Language: Português · English · ← Policies

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the game TOWER DEFENSE PVP ("app", "game", "we"), developed by Brazil Games Studio, manages player information. TOWER DEFENSE PVP is a real-time strategy, tower defense, and multiplayer duel game for Android (package com.brazilgamestudio.towerdefensepvp).

In short: you can play without providing your legal name, personal email, or phone number. Online matches employ anonymous or pseudonymous technical identifiers generated on your device for matchmaking and syncing tower placements and attack waves between opponents.

Need to delete records or request support for TOWER DEFENSE PVP?

See how to request deletion. You can also reset the game by clearing storage in Android settings.

1. What data the game handles

1.1 Multiplayer matches (PvP) and matchmaking

To connect two players in real-time battles, the game generates a technical session identifier (pseudonymous player ID). This ID coordinates tower decks, in-game nicknames, board placements, base HP, and network latency sync data between participants.

1.2 Progress, tower cards, and in-game currency

Your tower card collection, upgrade levels, trophy ranking, and virtual coins are saved on your device and may be backed up via cloud services for account continuity.

1.3 Advertising (Google AdMob / UMP)

The game may display ads (including optional rewarded ads to speed up chests or upgrades). We use Google AdMob with the Google UMP consent platform to manage user privacy permissions under LGPD and GDPR.

1.4 In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Gems, passes, or card pack purchases are executed via Google Play Billing. Credit card numbers and payment details are never directly handled or viewed by the game.

2. How we use your data

  • Match players in real-time and maintain competitive match integrity;
  • Store tower levels, rankings, and earned rewards;
  • Fulfill optional purchases made via Google Play;
  • Serve advertisements compliant with applicable privacy standards;
  • Analyze technical balance and resolve bugs.
