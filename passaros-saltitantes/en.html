PRIVACY POLICY

Language: Português · English · ← Policies

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the game PÁSSAROS SALTITANTES ("app", "game", "we"), developed by Brazil Games Studio, handles the information of its players. PÁSSAROS SALTITANTES is a flappy bird-style battle royale game for Android (package com.brazilgamesstudio.passarossaltitantes). Brazil Games Studio is responsible for processing data in the game services under its control; third-party services also have their own policies, listed below.

In short: you can play solo without creating a personal account or providing sensitive personal information. To sync your progress in the cloud, participate in online matches and leaderboards, an optional account is used based on a 12-character recovery code generated on your device, without requiring a personal email or Google account login. Online features, ads, purchases, and support requests may involve technical data processing. The Google account used for Google Play purchases is separate from your game account.

Want to delete your PÁSSAROS SALTITANTES account or data?

See how to request account and data deletion. You do not need to have the game installed to start a request.

1. What data the game handles

1.1 Game account, cloud save, and multiplayer

Online features use Google Firebase Authentication, Firebase Realtime Database, and Cloud Firestore / Remote Config. When connecting to online services, the game pseudonymously generates a 12-character recovery code on your device to enable cloud saving and account recovery across multiple devices.

DataPurpose
12-character recovery code and technical Firebase User ID (UID)Authenticate, sync cloud progress, and allow game account recovery on other devices
Nickname and match data (distance, scores, elapsed time, and game state)Display leaderboard rankings, match players, and sync multiplayer sessions
Cloud save progress (unlocked birds, coins, cosmetics, and achievements)Maintain and restore player unlocks and currency across sessions and devices
Technical connection parameters (IP address and authentication logs)Security, session maintenance, and fraud prevention within Firebase

The recovery code is a pseudonymous access credential. The game uses this code to derive technical credentials in Firebase. Keep your 12-character code in a safe place and do not share it.

1.2 Ads and privacy choices

The game uses Google AdMob and the consent platform Google UMP. These services may process IP addresses (including for coarse location estimation), Android advertising IDs, app set IDs, and device/system information for ad serving, measurement, and fraud prevention.

1.3 In-app purchases

In-app purchases and coin packs are processed directly by Google Play Billing. The integration handles product identifiers, purchase state, and transaction tokens to validate and grant virtual items to the player.

We do not receive your full credit card details, banking information, or Google account password.

1.4 Local progress and storage

Your device stores audio settings, graphics preferences, controls, language, mission progress, unlocked bird collection, and local coins. When connected to the internet, progress is synchronized with the cloud via Firebase Firestore.

1.5 Support contact and website access

If you contact us via email, we process your email address, provided name, and message to handle support requests. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical connection details (such as IP address).

2. How we use data

  • Provide the pseudonymous game account and cloud save progress;
  • Operate online leaderboards and multiplayer matches;
  • Maintain player preferences and deliver purchased virtual items;
  • Serve and measure ads based on your selected consent options;
  • Process in-app purchases via Google Play Billing;
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and respond to support requests.

3. Who data is shared with

We use Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Realtime Database, Remote Config), Google AdMob & UMP for ads, Google Play for purchases, and GitHub Pages for hosting this policy.

5. Retention, rights, and deletion

5.1 Local data

You can clear local data through your Android application settings or by uninstalling the game.

5.2 Online data

Account and cloud save data remain in Firebase until deletion is requested.

5.3 Requesting account and data deletion

PÁSSAROS SALTITANTES — Brazil Games Studio

Request deletion via email

You can also write directly to brazilgamesstudio@gmail.com, with the subject PÁSSAROS SALTITANTES — Account and data deletion.

  1. Provide your 12-character recovery code (if available) or in-game nickname.
  2. Do not send payment info or personal passwords. Minimal security verification may be requested.
  3. We will respond via email confirming the status and timeline of data deletion from Firebase.

6. Children

PÁSSAROS SALTITANTES is not directed to children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe data was inadvertently provided, please contact us for deletion.

7. Security and changes

Service communications use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We may update this policy to reflect future game updates.
