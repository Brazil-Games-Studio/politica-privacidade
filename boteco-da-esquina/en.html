BOTECO DA ESQUINA

PRIVACY POLICY

Language: Português · English · ← Policies

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the game BOTECO DA ESQUINA ("app", "game", "we"), developed by Brazil Games Studio, handles the information of its players. BOTECO DA ESQUINA is a collection of Brazilian bar games (truco, dominoes, sueca, cacheta, pife, mau-mau, tranca, bozó and checkers) for Android (package com.brazilgamestudio.botecodaesquina).

In short: there is no account, login, advertising, in-app purchase or tracking. We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or microphone. Playing against the bots works offline and sends no data. Only multiplayer (online or over Wi-Fi) exchanges match information with the other players.

Want to delete or learn more about your BOTECO DA ESQUINA data?

See how to ask. Since there is no account, the game's data stays on your device and you can erase it yourself.

1. What data the game handles

1.1 Data kept only on your device

Progress, statistics, achievements, bottle caps (the in-game currency, with no monetary value, which cannot be bought or exchanged for money), cosmetic store items, your character's look and nickname, and settings (sound, time of day, rain, language, difficulty) are stored in the game's own files on your device. The game also keeps there a random identifier and a secret key of the device, generated by the game itself and used only for multiplayer (item 1.2). Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings erases all of it.

1.2 Online multiplayer (rooms over the internet)

When you create or join an online room, the game connects to the Brazil Games Studio room server (party.hipercombo.com.br) over an encrypted connection (TLS). The server only relays messages between the players in the room; the game rules run on the device of whoever created the room.

This data lives in the server's memory while the room exists: there is no player database and no match history. The room disappears when everyone leaves. There is no text or voice chat, only pre-defined emotes. The server may log technical connection data (such as the IP address) for as long as needed to operate and protect the service.

1.3 Local multiplayer (Wi-Fi)

In local Wi-Fi mode, the game announces the room on your local network with the code, the room name, the game, the format, the number of players and the local IP address and port of the device that created the room, so other devices on the same network can find it. The devices connect directly to each other. None of this goes over the internet or through our servers. The local network connection is not encrypted: use networks you trust.

1.4 What the game does not collect

We do not collect real name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files, audio or microphone, payment data, advertising ID or crash reports. We do not use analytics or advertising tools.

1.5 Contacting support and visiting this page

If you contact us by email, we will handle your email address and message to reply. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical connection data (such as IP address).

2. Android permissions

  • Internet and network state: online multiplayer;
  • Wi-Fi state and Wi-Fi multicast: finding and announcing rooms on the local network.

The game does not request microphone, camera, location, contacts or storage permissions.

3. How we use the data

  • Make multiplayer work (rooms, matches, drops and returns);
  • Keep your progress and preferences on your device;
  • Operate, protect and prevent abuse of the room server;
  • Answer support and privacy requests.

We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

4. Who the data is shared with

Multiplayer data is seen by the other players in the same room (item 1.2) and passes through the Brazil Games Studio room server. We do not share data with advertisers or analytics companies. The game is distributed through Google Play and this page is hosted on GitHub Pages.

5. Third-party services

  • GitHub — GitHub General Privacy Statement

6. Retention, rights and deletion

6.1 Local data

You can remove the game's data by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the game.

6.2 Multiplayer data

Room data is not kept after the room ends. Since there is no account, we have no profile of yours to delete; if you believe some of your data stayed with us, ask below.

6.3 Request information or deletion of data

BOTECO DA ESQUINA — Brazil Games Studio

Write by email

You can also write directly to brazilgamesstudio@gmail.com, with the subject BOTECO DA ESQUINA — Data and privacy.

  1. Tell us the nickname you used and, if possible, the room code and the date of the match.
  2. Do not send payment data or passwords. We may ask for minimal security confirmations.
  3. We will reply by email telling you what was done and the timeframes.

You also have the rights provided by Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD), which we handle through the same contact.

7. Children

BOTECO DA ESQUINA is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. The nickname is chosen by the player; we recommend not using a real name. The game shows beer glasses and bottles in its scenery. If you are a legal guardian and believe a child's data was provided, contact us for deletion.

8. Security and changes

The connection to the room server over the internet is encrypted (TLS). We may update this policy to reflect changes to the game; the date at the top shows the last update and the new version stays at this same address.

9. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: brazilgamesstudio@gmail.com
