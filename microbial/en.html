MICROBIAL

PRIVACY POLICY

Language: Português · English · ← Policies

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the game MICROBIAL ("app", "game", "we"), developed by Brazil Games Studio, handles player information. MICROBIAL is a microscopic cellular survival and evolution arcade game for Android (package com.brazilgamestudio.microbial).

In short: you can play without registration, user accounts, or submitting personal data. Your cell evolution stages, unlocked mutations, and achievements are saved directly on your Android device. Advertising and optional in-app purchases adhere to standard Google Play privacy guidelines.

Want to delete data or ask questions about MICROBIAL?

See how to request data deletion. You can also delete local gameplay data directly through your Android device settings.

1. What data the game handles

1.1 Cellular progress and local storage

The game stores cell mutations, biomass points, completed stages, and audio/control preferences locally. These files stay strictly on your device and are not uploaded to any private servers.

1.2 Advertising (Google AdMob / UMP)

To support free gameplay, ads may be delivered via Google AdMob. AdMob uses the Google UMP consent platform to honor your choices under LGPD/GDPR, processing technical device parameters and advertising identifiers for ad delivery and fraud prevention.

1.3 In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional in-game purchases (such as ad removal or cosmetic perks) are handled securely by Google Play. We do not collect or store any financial or credit card information.

1.4 Support messages

When contacting our support email, your sender address and message content are used exclusively to assist you.

2. Purposes of data processing

  • Provide core gameplay mechanics, cell evolution, and high score tracking;
  • Serve non-personalized or personalized ads depending on your consent preferences;
  • Fulfill support requests and maintain game reliability.

3. Third-party disclosures

  • Google (AdMob, Google Play) — Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob — AdMob Play Data Disclosure
  • GitHub Pages (Hosting for this policy) — GitHub Privacy Statement

4. Data retention and deletion

4.1 On your device

You can delete your progress immediately at any time by going to Settings → Apps → MICROBIAL → Storage → Clear Data on your Android device.

5. Children's privacy

MICROBIAL is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Parents or guardians may contact us to request the immediate removal of any inadvertent data.
