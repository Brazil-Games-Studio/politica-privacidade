SPECTRUM

PRIVACY POLICY

Language: Português · English · ← Policies

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the game SPECTRUM ("app", "game", "we"), developed by Brazil Games Studio, handles the information of its players. SPECTRUM is a black-and-white endless runner for Android (package org.brazilgamesstudio.spectrum).

In short: the game works with no sign-up, no login and no email. We do not collect your real name, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. The only data that leaves your device is what's needed for the optional online ranking and for ads.

1. What data the game handles

1.1 Online ranking (optional)

If you play while connected to the internet, the game sends your run to an online leaderboard hosted on Google Firebase (Realtime Database). In that case, the following is handled:

DataPurpose
The nickname you choose (up to 12 characters)Show who is on the leaderboard
Run distance, steps and coinsRank the board and check the score is physically possible (anti-cheat)
Game account identifier (derived from a random code generated on your device)Tell your score apart from other players'

The identifier comes from a random recovery code generated on your device (you can save it to restore your account on another phone). It's a random code, not linked to your identity or your Google account. The nickname is chosen by you and is public on the leaderboard.

1.2 Ads

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. For that, AdMob may collect and use the Android advertising ID and technical device information (model, OS, language, ad interactions) to serve and measure ads. This data is processed by Google under its own policy (link in section 4).

1.3 "Remove ads" purchase

The purchase to remove ads is processed by Google Play (Play Billing). We do not receive or store payment data (card, etc.) — that stays with Google.

1.4 Data that stays only on your device

High score, coins, settings (sound, language), mission progress and an internal game key are stored locally on the device and are not sent to us. They are deleted when you uninstall the game.

2. How we use the data

  • Operate the online ranking (global, weekly and revived runs);
  • Prevent fraudulent scores (run validation);
  • Serve and measure ads;
  • Process the optional "remove ads" purchase.

We do not sell your data and do not use ranking data for advertising.

3. Who data is shared with

Only with providers needed to run the game: Google Firebase (ranking and anonymous identification), Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play (purchases). Your nickname and distance appear publicly on the leaderboard, inside the game and on a public leaderboard website, if available.

4. Third-party services

  • Google (Firebase, AdMob, Play) — Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services — policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Retention and deletion

Local data is gone when you uninstall the game. Ranking data (nickname + score + anonymous ID) stays on Firebase while the leaderboard exists. Since there is no login, to request removal of your score from the ranking, contact us (section 8) with the nickname you used; we'll locate and delete the record.

6. Children

SPECTRUM is not directed at children under 13 and does not request personal data. If you are a guardian and believe a child submitted data (for example, a nickname) to the ranking, contact us so we can remove it.

7. Security and changes

We take reasonable measures to protect data, but no system is 100% secure. We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be reflected on this same page.

8. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy and your data: brazilgamesstudio@gmail.com
