PRIVACY POLICY

Language: Português · English · ← Policies

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the game SPECTRUM ("app", "game", "we"), developed by Brazil Games Studio, handles the information of its players. SPECTRUM is a black-and-white endless runner for Android (package org.brazilgamesstudio.spectrum). Brazil Games Studio is responsible for processing data in the game services under its control; third-party services also have their own policies, listed below.

In short: you can play solo without signing in with a Google account. With a connection, the game creates a guest account by itself in the Hipercombo account service (hipercombo.com.br), with a nickname, without asking for your personal email; online leaderboards and Versus use it. Signing in with Google is optional and links the game to your Hipercombo account, with progress saved to the cloud. Online features, ads, purchases and support requests may involve data processing. The Google account used for Google Play purchases is separate from your game account.

Want to delete your SPECTRUM account or data?

In the game, under OPTIONS → ACCOUNT → delete data / account, or on the web, at hipercombo.com.br/conta/excluir. Deletion is immediate. See the details. You do not need to have the game installed to delete on the web.

1. What data the game handles

1.1 Game account and online leaderboards

The account, leaderboards, Versus and cloud save use the Hipercombo account service (the api-apps.hipercombo.com.br API, with data stored on Supabase). On first use with a connection, the game creates a guest account in the background (an anonymous account, with no name or email) with a generated nickname, which you can change. Run scores are sent to the leaderboards with that account. In OPTIONS → ACCOUNT, you can sign in with Google: the guest's progress moves to your Hipercombo account, and Google provides it with the data described in the Hipercombo Privacy Policy (name, email and profile photo). The same Hipercombo account works on the hipercombo.com.br site and in SwipeQuiz.

The guest account identifies the player pseudonymously and must not be treated as completely anonymous data. The access token stays on the device and does not appear on leaderboards. The game's statistics and achievements may also appear, as a display copy, on your Hipercombo profile.

Data from the old SPECTRUM stored in Google Firebase (accounts, nicknames, recovery codes, leaderboard scores and Versus) was not migrated to the Hipercombo account and will be deleted when the game's Firebase is shut down. Only the progress stored on the device itself is imported by the current version.

Nicknames and scores are public on leaderboards; accessible records also contain technical identifiers and run data. Avoid using your real name, phone number or other personal information in a nickname. With an active account, offline runs may remain in a local queue for later submission, including when you reopen the game.

The Hipercombo account service and Supabase may also process IP addresses, connection client information and technical logs for authentication, security and abuse prevention.

1.2 Versus and invitations

When you create or join a duel, the Hipercombo account service receives participant identifiers, nicknames, character appearance with equipped cosmetics, the invitation code, creation and joining times, game version and technical match data, and each player's results: distance, simulation steps and run coins.

Anyone who knows the invitation code and accesses the service can view match data; do not treat it as a private conversation. The six-character invitation code is different from your account access token. COPY puts the invitation code on your device's clipboard; you decide whom to send it to in another app. The game does not read your address book or send messages to your contacts.

1.3 Ads and privacy choices

The game uses Google AdMob and the Google UMP consent platform. These services may process IP addresses (including to estimate approximate location), the Android advertising ID, app set identifiers and other applicable identifiers, device and system information, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance data. Purposes include serving and measuring ads, analyzing operation and preventing fraud, depending on the service and identifier used.

In OPTIONS → ADS, you can revisit the choice between ACCEPT PERSONALIZED and ESSENTIAL ONLY. The latter requests non-personalized ads; it does not mean no ads or no technical data processing. Google may show its own consent form where applicable. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID through the controls available in Android settings.

Rewarded ads require the player's choice. Test versions may use demo ads, but that does not eliminate technical data processing by the integrated services.

1.4 In-app purchases

Coin packs are processed through Google Play Billing. The game also recognizes compatible previous purchases, including the ad-removal entitlement. The integration handles product identifiers, prices and offers, purchase status and transaction tokens to deliver products, acknowledge or consume purchases and query recoverable entitlements. Purchase data is not published on leaderboards or sent by the game to the score database. With a Google account, the tokens of purchases already credited (so the same purchase is not credited twice) and the ad-removal entitlement are part of the progress saved to the cloud.

We do not receive full card details, banking credentials or your Google account password. Payments and purchase history held by Google Play follow Google's rules and policy. Joining a closed test does not automatically make purchases free; purchases without charges require the appropriate Google Play testing setup and test payment instrument.

1.5 Local progress, achievements and backups

Your device stores sound, language, control and privacy settings, coin balance, power-up and cosmetic inventory, missions, statistics, achievements, local high score, game credentials, queued scores and duel references. There are exceptions to local storage: scores and run data are sent to leaderboards when you have an account, and equipped appearance is shared in duels, as explained above.

Achievements give no coin rewards. Their progress stays on the device and is sent to Google Play Games when Play Games sign-in is available. Signing in with the same Google account on another device restores the progress saved to the cloud; the guest account has no cloud save. Recoverable purchase entitlements are queried separately through Google Play.

The app allows Android data backup and transfer. Depending on the device and user settings, the operating system may include local files in backups or transfer them to another device. Therefore, we do not promise that uninstalling the game deletes every copy or prevents future restoration.

1.6 Support contact and access to this page

If you contact us, we process your email address, provided name, message and any attachments to respond, identify your request and address your rights. Send only what is necessary; do not send passwords or card details.

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may process IP addresses and technical connection and browser information to provide and protect the site. These pages do not include our own advertising or visitor analytics scripts.

5. Retention, rights and deletion

5.1 Local data

You can remove installation data through Android storage settings or by uninstalling the game. Backups and transferred copies must be managed through the operating system or the responsible provider. Clearing data or uninstalling does not delete your account or online records (for that, see section 5.3), or purchase history held by Google Play.

5.2 Online data

Authentication data, profiles, nickname reservations, leaderboards and duels remain in online services until deletion (section 5.3) or administrative cleanup. The end of a leaderboard period or expiration of an invitation does not automatically erase its records. Support records are retained to handle and follow up on requests and any applicable obligations.

Providers' technical logs, backups and purchase records may have their own retention periods, described in the policies in section 4. A request to the studio covers data under our control; it does not delete your Google or GitHub account or records those providers must retain independently of the game.

5.3 Delete your account and data

In the game: under OPTIONS → ACCOUNT → delete data / account. On the web: at https://hipercombo.com.br/conta/excluir, signing in with your Google account. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. There are two options:

  • This game's data (DELETE SPECTRUM DATA): deletes from the server the progress saved to the cloud, the game's statistics and achievements shown on your Hipercombo profile, submitted matches that have not yet turned into XP, your leaderboard scores, the Versus duels you played (the other player loses that duel from their history) and the link between your devices and SPECTRUM. The Hipercombo account, the site and SwipeQuiz remain.
  • The whole Hipercombo account (DELETE HIPERCOMBO ACCOUNT, with a second confirmation): deletes the account with everything linked to it on the hipercombo.com.br site, in SPECTRUM and in SwipeQuiz.

With a guest account, the screen only has DELETE MY DATA, which deletes the whole guest account and its data on the server. When you delete in the game, the device progress is also deleted (high score, coins, including purchased ones, items and scores); language, sound, controls and the ads choice remain, and a purchased ad removal stays valid through Google Play. When you delete on the web, progress saved on a device stays there until it is deleted in the game or the game is uninstalled.

Account deletion prevents recovery and does not constitute a purchase refund. Payment requests follow Google Play's channels and rules. You can also contact us to request confirmation of processing, access, correction and other rights under Brazil's LGPD and applicable law, including withdrawal of consent where relevant.

6. Children

SPECTRUM is not directed at children under 13. We do not request your legal name, personal email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location to play (signing in with Google is optional); this does not eliminate the processing of identifiers and other data described in this policy. If you are a guardian and believe a child provided data to the game, contact us for assistance and deletion.

7. Security and changes

Communications with integrated online services use HTTPS/TLS. The Hipercombo account service requires the account access token to protect operations; leaderboard and invitation data remain accessible as described in this policy. We do not promise absolute anonymity, end-to-end encryption or 100% security. Protect your device.

We may update this policy to reflect actual changes to the game and its services. The date at the top indicates the latest update. Where changes require fresh consent, it must be requested through the appropriate flow, not assumed merely because this page has been updated.
