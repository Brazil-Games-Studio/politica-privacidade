PRIVACY POLICY

Language: Português · English · ← Policies

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the game FUGA DO CANÁRIO ("app", "game", "we"), developed by Brazil Games Studio, handles player data. FUGA DO CANÁRIO is a casual endless flyer, obstacle-dodging adventure with a charming canary for Android (package com.brazilgamestudio.fugadocanario).

In short: you can play freely without creating accounts, logins, or entering personal information. Your gameplay records (highest flight distance, collected seeds/coins, and canary skins) are saved directly on your device. Advertisements are served safely, complying with family protection and privacy regulations (LGPD/GDPR/COPPA).

Questions about privacy or data deletion in FUGA DO CANÁRIO?

See how to request assistance. Since the game stores data locally on your device, you can delete it anytime through Android settings.

1. Data processed by the game

1.1 Flight records and local progress

Flight distances, high scores, collected coins/seeds, unlocked canary cosmetics, and sound effects preferences are kept solely on the player's device.

1.2 Advertising and consent (Google AdMob / UMP)

To support free access, the app shows ads through Google AdMob. The Google UMP framework manages user privacy choices (LGPD/GDPR). AdMob processes technical device identifiers and ad interaction metrics to deliver ads and mitigate fraud.

1.3 In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional in-game cosmetic purchases or coin packs are fulfilled directly via Google Play. We never have access to your credit card or financial account numbers.

2. Purposes

  • Provide core endless runner gameplay, saving high scores and bird skins;
  • Fulfill cosmetic purchases made through the official store;
  • Serve ads following selected privacy preferences;
  • Deliver customer support when requested.

3. Third-party partners

4. How to delete your data

To clear stored data on your smartphone, navigate to: Settings → Apps → FUGA DO CANÁRIO → Storage → Clear Data.
